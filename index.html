HOW I BUILD

From a messy input to a shipped, running product.

01

Ingest

Take the messy input — notes, PDFs, raw feeds, past results pages — and turn it into structured data.

02

Understand

Wire in an LLM — Gemini, GPT, or Claude — to extract, classify, generate, or reason over that data.

03

Build

Ship it as a real product with Next.js, Node, or Flutter — not a notebook, something people can open and use.

04

Automate

Wrap it in a workflow — n8n, background jobs, scheduled tasks — so it keeps running without me in the loop.
